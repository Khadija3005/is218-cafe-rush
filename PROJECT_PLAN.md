# Café Rush - Creative Project Plan


**Course:** IS218-003 | **Stage:** Review 2 - Plan/SPEC | **Updated:** November 19, 2026


## Review 2 checkpoint

Café Rush is a single-player game where players sign up or sign in, make drinks for five orders, save a server-verified score, and sign out. The full project includes private score history, a best score, and account maintenance.

**Planned milestone:** Build an untimed round using Flask and SQLite. Compare recipe conditionals with a recipe lookup using the same tests.

**Planned check (unrun):** Complete five orders with four correct drinks and one incorrect drink. Sign out and back in, then verify that exactly one 80-point result appears in the player's private history.

**Peer review (pending):** Ask a classmate to predict the saved result and record their feedback, the decision, any change, verification and reviewer retry in the existing issue.

## Two concepts and choice


| Concept | Useful interaction | Saved record | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Café Rush - selected | Make customer drinks | Verified round score and order outcomes | Three recipes, five orders, single player |
| Study Session Tracker | Enter study subject and minutes | Personal study history | Manual logging and totals |


Café Rush was selected because making drinks and completing orders is a more playful project. This is a preference and scope decision, not evidence that other players prefer it.


## Audience and complete promise


Audience: college students wanting a quick casual game. Register and log in, play a five-order café round, save the server-verified result, view a private score history and best score, update display name/password, and log out.
